How It Works

Our 5-Day Painting Process

A structured, production-based workflow that keeps your home painting project on schedule, clean, and professionally managed from start to finish.

Day 1
Prep & Protection

We protect floors, cover furniture, mask all surfaces, complete minor wall repairs, sand, clean dust, and prepare the entire home for production painting.

Day 2
Ceilings & First Coats

We begin production painting — completing ceilings and starting first coats on walls with an organized, room-by-room system.

Day 3
Walls & Corrections

We complete all wall coats, inspect coverage, correct any imperfections, and ensure surfaces look consistent and uniform throughout.

Day 4
Trim, Doors & Detail Work

We focus on baseboards, doors, frames, window trim, and all detail finishing — clean lines, sharp cuts, and precise application.

Day 5
Touch-Ups & Walkthrough

We remove all protection, clean the entire work area, complete final touch-ups, and walk through the finished project with you.

Application Method

Roller or Spray Finish Available

Every home is different. Depending on the project, we can use traditional roll-and-brush application or a professional spray system for selected surfaces such as doors, trim, and large open areas.

Standard

Standard Roll Finish

  • Great for occupied homes
  • Controlled, precise application
  • Ideal for walls and ceilings
  • Easier future touch-ups
Premium

Professional Spray Finish

  • Smooth, premium factory-like finish
  • Excellent for doors and trim
  • Fast production in prepared spaces
  • Requires detailed masking and protection
